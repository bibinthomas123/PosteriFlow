# Certification reports older than 2026-11-05: read with care

Three bugs were found on 2026-11-05 (analysis/distance_realdata/):

1. Real-noise training signals were ~1.4x too loud (highpass gain missing from recolouring).
2. Right ascension on real events was rotated by GMST(t) - GMST(GPS_REF).
3. The LVK comparison reweighted to p(d) ~ d^2 instead of a comoving prior.

Every model trained with `real_noise_prob > 0` before commit 787d524 has bug 1 in its weights.
That includes the production BNS (lean_npe_bns_v5) and NSBH (lean_npe_nsbh_v5ff) models. Their
real-noise coverage and NLL gates compared them against equally wrong validation data, so a pass
there does not show real-event amplitude is right.

Reports regenerated with the fixed data and the new real-path gates (amplitude, sky, distance):
lean_npe_bbh_v6, lean_npe_bbh_v7, lean_npe_bbh_v6psd64, lean_npe_bbh_v6psd64pol, and every model
trained afterwards. Their real-noise and real-path gates are expected to FAIL for models trained
before the fix, because they now read the true amplitude. That is the correct verdict.

All other reports here are historical and were not regenerated.
